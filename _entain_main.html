▲ How to think about this account — per DJ, 19 Aug 2026

Treat Ladbrokes and neds as Australian brands that roll into Entain Australia, run out of Brisbane. The UK is a separate business. Entain ANZ's own description backs this up — it calls itself "the home of Ladbrokes, neds, TAB, betcha, Trackside and Sport Nation" and says it "started our journey as Ladbrokes Australia and neds."

And the decisive point: the technology is paid for out of Australia. That makes Entain ANZ a real buying centre with its own budget, not a regional office waiting on London. It is also why the two separate "Ladbrokes" and "neds" line items on the target list should be worked as one Brisbane account rather than three.

Why the timing is good

  • New Zealand opens to online gaming in 2027 — casino regulation starts at the beginning of the year, the market is capped at 15 licences, and Entain has signalled it will apply for three, including its TAB monopoly.
  • NZ is already growing 23% (21% online, 29% retail) and Australia rebounded 13% online.
  • Market-share gains in Australia are being credited to product — bet builder tools and native apps — so this is an organisation that invests in customer-facing technology.
  • Andrew Vouris became ANZ CEO in 2025, from 17 years across Tabcorp and Entain. A local CEO with an operating background.
  • They have a named Head of Contact Centres. Most accounts on this list do not.

Why it has gone nowhere

  • The global opportunity "Entain NN – Nov 25" was Closed Disqualified on 22 April 2026. Nobody has recorded why.
  • The ANZ account is owned by Decagon RevOps — an integration user. In practice, unowned.
  • Its only two contacts are Brodie Mcintyre and Lily Sommers, both Senior Product Manager, both untouched since 30 April 2026.
  • Hursh Rughani owns Entain plc with 30 contacts, so the global relationship sits elsewhere and nothing has been handed across.
  • Entain carried three ZoomInfo intent signals — more than any other account on DJ's list — and we never pulled them. Credits are now exhausted.
▲ Still worth ten minutes: why did the global deal die?

"Entain NN – Nov 25" was Closed Disqualified on 22 April with no reason recorded, by Alex Lyons on the global account. Because ANZ funds its own technology, a UK disqualification does not block Australia — but the reason still matters. If it died on an in-house build or a group-wide platform standard, we will meet the same argument in Brisbane. Ask Alex Lyons and Hursh Rughani before spending founder time.

▲ The strategic read

Wagering has the same spiky-volume shape as ticketing — race days and major events, not steady demand. Layer on Australia's responsible-gambling regime, where operators must demonstrate interaction and self-exclusion compliance, and the pitch is guardrails plus surge capacity rather than deflection. The 2027 New Zealand licences turn that from a cost argument into a growth-readiness argument, which is a much better conversation to have with a CEO who has just been handed a growing business.
