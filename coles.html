▲ "Internal build" means Microsoft, and it is much further along than the CRM implies

Coles has a five-year strategic partnership with Microsoft covering cloud, AI and edge — explicitly to build an AI-as-a-Service platform and modernise its IT estate. Over 70% of Coles' data is already in the cloud. Azure Stack HCI runs in 500+ stores. And a generative AI assistant has already been rolled out to all 115,000 team members. Microsoft AI has also been applied to flybuys customer experience. This is not a skunkworks project we can out-execute — it is a funded platform strategy with a hyperscaler behind it.

Sierra and the retail narrative

  • BDR feedback: Sierra perceived as stronger in retail, with Coles and Woolworths named as Sierra's territory.

▲ Where Decagon actually differentiates here

Yass engaged on observability, trace view and Watchtower — not on containment. That is the signal worth trusting. Coles can already build an assistant with Microsoft; what a hyperscaler platform partnership does not hand them is operator-level control, auditability and guardrails over an agent that talks to customers. Lead there, and complement Azure rather than attack it — the same discipline that applied to Google at Woolworths.
