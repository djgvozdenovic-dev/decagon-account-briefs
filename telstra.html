● The hook that makes this a founder conversation

On 13 August, Telstra's remuneration report tied 5% of FY27 executive short-term incentives to 11 internal AI projects — Vicki Brady and her leadership team must hit targets in at least 8 of the 11 to unlock it. One of the named projects is using AI to reduce how often customers are transferred between call-centre staff. That is our product, written into the CEO's bonus scheme, in public, three weeks ago.

The good

  • Deutsche Telekom is landing hard. Keelan on the DT reference: "finally talked to someone who's actually getting this done in production… most are lucky to have had success with one or two use cases. DT was eye opening."
  • Already budgeted. Not a business-case exercise.
  • Commercials presented with no pushback (19 Aug), and the collections use case landed.
  • Four meetings in three days. Keelan: "Make sure to bring anything you have to me. Onsites, new ideas, news."
  • Ben Carmichael has already shipped a branded demo site with a V1 agent over ~19,000 scraped Telstra knowledge articles.

The problem

  • Four named competitors: Sierra, ElevenLabs, Salesforce (~$50M/yr — their biggest ANZ account, will flood it with people) and the Accenture JV itself.
  • Amy Childs holds the budget and we have never spoken to her. Zero recorded activity.
  • Keelan Connor is not even a Salesforce contact — the single most important person in the deal exists only in Slack.
  • October is a cliff. Formal vendor engagement opens then; if it becomes an RFP we enter probity and lose the ability to engage outside backchannels.
  • No Australian entity, and onshore data/model residency will likely be mandatory.
▲ Calibration

Mike's read was "this is our deal to lose" — but he flagged it himself as "my happy ears talking." Treat the optimism as untested: MEDDPICC is 0, the economic buyer is unconfirmed, and the decision criteria are still unwritten.
